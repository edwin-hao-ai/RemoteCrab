Digital Supporter — $1

Not revenue. The point of this tier is to get on the list.

  • Every development letter, as they happen
  • Your name in the credits
  • A vote on the public roadmap

No product. This tier exists because the $39 tier needs a floor to be measured against, and because the cheapest possible backer is the cheapest possible spreader.

Founding Member — $39 (1,000 only)

This is the tier the campaign is about.

  • Perpetual Pro licence, personal. A $99 licence at 39% off. No subscription, no renewal, no recurring fee — the same promise the free tier makes.
  • Every stretch goal we unlock, for the life of the licence
  • A vote on the public roadmap
  • Beta channel access during development

Why perpetual? Because we have no servers and no cloud. There is no hosting bill to pass on to you, so there is nothing to charge you for after the first year. If a future version of the product ever does have a running cost, we will say so on this page before it happens — not after.

Why 1,000 only? A perpetual licence is a person we support forever. Capping it keeps that promise honest.

Founder's Edition — $149 (200 only)

Everything in Founding Member, plus:

  • Three perpetual Pro seats — yours, plus two you can hand to colleagues. Genuinely useful if you run more than one Mac.
  • 3× roadmap vote weight
  • A Founder badge in the app
  • A numbered CNC aluminium badge, milled to order, with your backer number on it. This is the only physical thing in the campaign, and it is the reason this tier exists.

The badge is why this tier ships about a month after the $39 tier. It is a small milled aluminium plate, not electronics, so it is low-risk — but it is a real object being made and sent to you, and we would rather quote you three months and hit it than one month and miss it.
