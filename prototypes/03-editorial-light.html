— 01

Visual Language

Warm cream background (#FAFAF8), generous typography, single orange accent. This is the "designer's portfolio" look — Linear, Arc, Notion, Things 3. Light variant leans into editorial magazine quality. The orange accent pops especially well on warm light surfaces.

— 02

Target User

Designers, writers, indie devs, content creators — anyone who values craft and aesthetic restraint. The light variant especially appeals to people with creative or print backgrounds.

— 03

Trade-offs

Less "system tool" feel, more "indie product" feel. May not appeal to enterprise or pro broadcast users. Strong brand identity means strong opinions — not everyone will love the orange.
