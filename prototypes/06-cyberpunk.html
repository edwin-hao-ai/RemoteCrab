VISUAL_LANGUAGE

Terminal-aesthetic with neon cyan + magenta. Scanline overlays, glowing borders, blinking cursors, bracketed ALL_CAPS labels, JetBrains Mono + Orbitron fonts. Inspired by Cyberpunk 2077 UI, Mr. Robot, Watch Dogs, retro CRT terminals. Camera viewfinder has corner brackets like a security feed.

TARGET_USER

Developers, gamers, cyberpunk enthusiasts, anyone who uses terminal daily. People who want their tools to feel like they belong in a hacker movie. Strong niche appeal — polarizing but memorable.

TRADE-OFFS

Highly polarizing. Definitely not for enterprise or general consumers. High implementation cost (custom fonts, scanline effects, glows). But: instant brand recognition if you find the right audience.
