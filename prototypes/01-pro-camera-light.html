Visual Language

Off-white surfaces with crisp typography. Technical readouts stay in SF Mono. Camera viewfinder intentionally stays dark — it's a video preview, regardless of system theme. Inspired by broadcast hardware panels rendered in daylight.

Target User

Same power users as dark variant, but with daytime/lit-environment preference. Designers, color-accurate workflows, office settings. People who find dark mode eye-straining in daylight.

Trade-offs

Less "broadcast gear" energy — the white surfaces feel more clinical. Some elements (pills, modifiers) need careful shadow/border balance to feel premium rather than washed out.
