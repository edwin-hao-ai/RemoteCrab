Visual Language

Translucent materials everywhere (vibrancy), gradient accents (System Blue → Indigo), generous whitespace. Refined SF Pro typography with large titles and small captions. Feels like Apple's own Pro apps — Final Cut, Logic, Motion.

Target User

Apple ecosystem users who value polish and native feel. Designers, creatives, executives using Mac mini/Mac Pro. People who'd pay premium for "it just feels right."

Trade-offs

Less information-dense. The translucent material aesthetic can blur readability for technical readouts. Less "broadcast equipment" energy — more "Apple keynote slides."
