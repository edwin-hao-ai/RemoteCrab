Visual Language

Real-world physical controls — metal gradients, inset/outset shadows that mimic machined aluminum, glowing indicator lights. Inspired by Blackmagic Camera, Filmic Pro, ARRI cinema cameras, and Things 3's skeuomorphic golden era. The accent is warm orange (like cinema equipment LEDs).

Target User

Cinematographers, filmmakers, video professionals who appreciate tactile aesthetics. People who like Filmic Pro, Blackmagic Camera, ProCam. Anyone who feels "skeuomorphism" is the most premium aesthetic.

Trade-offs

Polarizing — some users love this (it feels rich, crafted, intentional), others find it dated. Higher implementation cost (gradients, shadows, layers). Doesn't fit "modern flat" trends. But: instantly distinctive.
