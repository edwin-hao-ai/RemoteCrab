— 01

Visual Language

Zero accent color. Pure black on white. Every line, shape, and weight is intentional. This is the Apple-original aesthetic — watchOS faces, early iOS, Apple Notes, Things 3. Designed to feel like it could have shipped in 2007 and never look dated.

— 02

Target User

Design purists, minimalists, anyone tired of color noise. People who delete default apps because they have colored icons. The audience that said "Bring back the original Mac Pro" and meant it.

— 03

Trade-offs

Lowest implementation cost (no color tokens to manage). Highest visual restraint. Risk: feels generic, undifferentiated. Mitigated by typography confidence — SF Pro Display at large sizes is what makes this direction feel premium rather than bland.
