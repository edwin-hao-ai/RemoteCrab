Visual Language

This is the most "Apple-native" direction — uses the same vibrancy, materials, and color tiers Apple uses in their own apps. Looks like it could ship as a first-party Apple app. The light variant has the upper hand here: vibrancy materials really shine on bright surfaces.

Target User

Every Apple user. This is the "safe" choice — anyone who likes Apple's own apps will feel at home. Pairs especially well with macOS Sonoma's translucent design language.

Trade-offs

Less brand differentiation. Might feel like "another Settings panel" rather than a premium product. Easy to blend in, harder to stand out.
